## (b) Bindings
- **Tournament setup:** double hard-shell or high-wrap boots. **Recreational and learner setup:** a front boot plus a **rear toe plate (RTP)**. (Bart's: double boots are "locked-in… for advanced/tournament", the RTP is easier to get in and out of. Connelly packages are sold as "Double Tempest / Tempest w/ RTP".)
- The front boot sits over the middle of the ski. The rear boot or RTP is **right behind it**: a 1/16–1/2 in gap, **10–13 in from ankle to ankle** (Bart's, Waterskiers World).
- Both feet point forward along the ski. The front foot sometimes toes out slightly; the rear foot is angled a little more (about 10–15°).
- Skiers are **barefoot inside** the boots, or in thin neoprene socks. **There are no sneakers in slalom.**
- High-wrap or hard-shell boots come up over the ankle (a tall cuff, 15–20 cm, with laces or a power strap). An RTP is a toe pocket on a heel pad.
- **Game model:** a front high-wrap boot plus a rear high-wrap (double boots, the tournament look Scott described). Black shell, dark-grey liner, a teal power strap on the cuff. Front ankle 0.79 m from the tail, rear ankle 0.29 m behind it (11.4 in), rear foot turned 12°.

## (c) Stance and motion
Read from the keyframes (study only) and MOTION-BREAKDOWN §2.

**Pulling / load (keyframes 07, 08, 09):**
- A straight line from ankle to shoulder, leaning **40–50° from vertical, away from the boat**.
- **Hips up and forward over the front foot**, shoulders back, head up.
- **Front knee bent more** (about 35–45°) than the rear (about 25°). The rear leg is behind, with feet in tandem on the ski.
- The ski is on edge.
- Arms straight but relaxed, **handle low at the hips**, elbows near the vest.
- At the edge change the handle comes into the hip with elbows about 90°.

**Outbound glide (01, 10):** taller and stacked, lean drops from about 30° to 10°, both hands on the handle near the hip.

**Turn (02–05):**
- The outside hand releases and reaches forward toward the buoy.
- The body inclines **50–65° toward the buoy**, knees deeply bent, hips near the water.
- The ski edge is at 45–65°, the **tip is up** (the front 20–30% out of the water) and the ski pivots on the tail.
- The upper body counter-rotates against the ski.

**Reconnect (06):** the free hand returns to the handle, hips forward, shoulders square.

**Key tell:** the upper body does **not** turn rigidly with the ski. It keeps facing the boat through the pull and only counter-rotates into the turn.

**What the current N1 rider gets wrong:**
- Upright, side-by-side feet and sneakers.
- A flat slab ski.
- A capsule "vest".
- Hands at the chest, and lean coming only from rolling the whole root.

## (d) Gear
- **Vest:** a fitted **competition vest**: neoprene shell over **segmented ("siped") foam panels**, open slalom arm holes, a front zip and usually one buckle or strap (Radar Lyric: "tapered and segmented foam panels… 4-way stretch neoprene… slalom-specific arm openings").
  - Keyframes show black or navy vests with colour side panels.
  - **Game:** navy panels to match the boat blue, slate-grey side panels, a black zip strip, one black waist strap with a grey buckle.
- **Lower body:** board shorts, compression shorts or a shorty or full wetsuit. Keyframe 07 shows bright shorts over bare legs.
  - **Game:** Remy's cargo shorts reskinned as navy and teal board shorts (cargo bump map removed). The T-shirt is reskinned as a dark rash guard under the vest.
- **Gloves:** common (keyframes 04 and 07 show dark gloves). Game: dark grey gloves (hand tint) are optional and done as a texture tint.
- **No hat** and no sunglasses in course skiing.

## Pass 3 corrections (after the first comparison sheet)
- **Head and chest:** the head stays level, eyes forward or on the next buoy, and the chest stays up over the hips. Most of the lean comes from the ski's edge angle, not from bending back at the waist or neck. The rig's own lean is now 4–9°, and the head is levelled after all the torso edits.
- **Bindings:** rubber high-wraps are low, soft and close to the foot: roughly foot width (about 9–10 cm), wrapping 18–22 cm up the ankle, low over the toes. They're not a rigid ski-boot block.
- **Ski in the water:** at speed the tail and most of the deck run under the surface with the tip and rocker out. In turns the ski is rolled hard onto its edge (it carries the body angle). Top sheets are saturated colours, which read against dark water.
- **Spray:** every turn throws a sheet or wall of spray off the ski's base side (outside the turn), plus a rooster tail off the tail and churned foam behind. Its size grows with edge angle and speed.
