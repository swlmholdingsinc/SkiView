# Texture licenses (houses/textures), v2 detail pass (Oct 18, 2026)
Each lot has 4 textures, named by plat lot index (lot01 = northmost ... lot18 = southmost; no street numbers in texture names):
- `hv2_lotNN_albedo.jpg`: 2048 px body atlas (4x4 grid of 512 px cells; tiling tiles have wrap padding, and geometry is cut along repeat lines so the tiles repeat inside the atlas).
- `hv2_lotNN_normal.jpg`: matching OpenGL-convention normal atlas (Unity: Texture Type = Normal map).
- `hv2_lotNN_glass.jpg`: small dark-tint gradient for the opaque glass material.
- `hv2_lotNN_impostor.jpg`: LOD2 atlas (5 ortho views rendered from our own LOD0 model, plus 1 deck tile).

Sources of the atlas tiles:
- **Procedural (original, generated by `_scripts/texgen_v2.py`, no third-party content).** Lap, board-and-batten, shake and panel siding; stucco; architectural shingles; standing-seam metal roof; brick; pavers/caps; foliage; painted trim, frames and metal; water; lamp; entry doors (traditional and modern); garage doors (sectional, carriage, flush).
- **CC0 1.0 photo-scans, tinted per lot to the brief's hex colours.** No attribution required; downloaded Oct 17, 2026 (CT); see ../../LICENSES.md.
